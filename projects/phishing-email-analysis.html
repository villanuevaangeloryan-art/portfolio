Sample: Phishing Email Analysis

Sample page. This is placeholder content for previewing the layout. It is not a real project.

Objective

Learn to recognise phishing emails by examining sample messages and documenting the warning signs.

Overview

Each sample email is examined for the following:

  1. Sender and header details, such as the reply-to address and mail route.
  2. Links, checked against where they really lead.
  3. Attachments, checked before anything is opened.
  4. Pressure tactics, such as urgency or threats.
  5. A short write-up listing the indicators found and the next step an analyst would take.

Technologies used

  • Email headers Technical details inside every message that show who sent it and how it travelled.
  • VirusTotal Online service that checks links and files against many security scanners.
  • Incident investigation Recording indicators and deciding what should happen next.
